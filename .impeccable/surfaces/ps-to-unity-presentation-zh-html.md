## Scope and mode

`PS_To_Unity_Presentation_zh.html` is a Persuade-mode release report for live presentation and later self-guided reading.

## Audience and job

Non-technical presentation viewers must understand within seconds what the tool changes, then follow enough real workflow and proof to trust the result.

## Action, proof, and constraints

- Primary action: continue into the report; secondary action: open the full Chinese guide.
- Proof: real Photoshop-to-Unity mappings, the layer-naming tag vocabulary the tool actually parses, the four-step workflow, current version v2.13.4, a documented 43-to-26 pixel-dedup example, real degradation codes (`LAYOUT_CROSS_AXIS_DEGRADED`, `SCROLLBAR_ORPHAN_UNRESOLVED`), and the Phase 4.5 acceptance record including the two blocking defects found and fixed.
- Constraints: short Traditional Chinese copy, strict 60/30/10 color balance (mint never fills a whole section), no invented time savings, client counts, or success rates; standalone offline HTML.

## Direction and memorable moment

Use a release-result trajectory. The first viewport makes the mechanism visible as a Photoshop layer stack transforming into a Unity Prefab hierarchy — stacked, not side by side, because a 1180px container cannot hold two 18px-text windows beside the copy without truncating the layer names that are the whole point of the figure. The page then travels through problem (including the per-revision rework loop), semantic mapping, naming-as-spec, technology bridge, use, result, post-generation tooling, and safety guardrails.
